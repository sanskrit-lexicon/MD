## Relationship to MW

[Macdonell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Anthony_Macdonell) was Boden Professor of Sanskrit at Oxford, succeeding Monier-Williams. MD (1893) is a compact English-language dictionary primarily for Classical Sanskrit, contemporaneous with CAE (1891) and the later editions of AP90. It is not as exhaustive as MW but known for clarity of definition.
